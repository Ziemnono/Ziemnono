## About me

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, medical imaging, computational engineering and research software development. My experience spans clinical imaging research, patient-specific digital twins, interactive surgical simulation and the deployment of medical imaging and research data infrastructure.

I completed my PhD in Engineering Sciences as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral researcher at the University of Luxembourg, where I received the Excellent Doctoral Thesis Award.

## Research vision

My ambition is to bring **AI to the forefront of healthcare innovation** by bridging the gap between emerging technologies and clinical practice.

I am particularly interested in exploring generative AI, large language models, AI agents and multimodal learning to develop intelligent, secure and scalable healthcare tools. I am also interested in connecting AI with patient-specific simulation, digital twins and immersive technologies to support clinical decision-making, personalised medicine and healthcare automation.

## What I work on

- **Medical imaging & AI:** quantitative coronary CT, AI-assisted plaque phenotyping, cardiovascular risk modelling and imaging harmonisation.
- **Computational biomechanics:** cardiovascular haemodynamics, finite element analysis, patient-specific modelling and surgical simulation.
- **Research software & data engineering:** Python automation, REST APIs, clinical imaging biobanks, de-identification, database integration and reproducible analysis pipelines.
- **Cloud & research computing:** AWS, Linux, containerised services and distributed computational infrastructure.
- **Clinical technology translation:** AR/VR prototype deployment, including contributions to demonstrations at the opening of the Perich Centre for Robotics & Health Technology.
