# Hey, I'm Arnaud 👋

I'm an engineer and researcher based in Sydney, currently working at the University of Sydney.

I like building things that make complicated problems easier to explore — whether that's a simulation, a data pipeline or a small tool that saves someone hours of manual work.

### What you'll find here

| Project | What it's about |
| --- | --- |
| [Coronary CFD framework](https://github.com/Ziemnono/coronary-cfd-reductionist-framework) | Generating coronary geometries and analysing flow simulations in a reproducible way. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22106487) |

### Things I'm curious about

- Running AI models locally and experimenting with multimodal models and agents.
- Making research workflows more automated, reproducible and accessible.
- Connecting medical imaging, 3D simulation and immersive interfaces.
- Finding useful, practical applications of new technologies in healthcare.

Most of my work involves Python, Linux and Docker, with some numerical modelling, cloud infrastructure and plenty of troubleshooting along the way.

Outside of work, I enjoy volleyball and playing guitar.
